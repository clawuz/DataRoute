# Missing instruments (research list)

Licence rule: CC0 and CC BY are accepted. NC (non-commercial) is accepted for now because the music made with it is original, but every NC file carries `nc: true` in `docs/music-pack-world-ledger.json` and a "non-commercial licence" mark on `docs/credits.html`, and a deploy that includes NC files needs the owner's explicit yes. ND and unclear licences are not accepted. Keep a ledger entry (source, author, licence, url) for every sample that is taken.

**RESTRICTED (2026-10-28):** the Black Sea kemençe (KEMENCE_KARADENIZ) and the tulum (TULUM) are cut from two sound samples of the Turkish Music Portal (Türk Kültür Vakfı, http://www.turkishmusicportal.org). The portal's "Telif Hakkı ve Gizlilik" terms allow personal, non-commercial, educational or research use only, say the content must not be modified, and ask for written permission for any other use. The owner accepted that risk for the local pack (the music made with it is original; permission is to be asked if needed). Every such file carries `restricted: true, nc: true, terms: ...` in `docs/music-pack-world-ledger.json` and a "restricted: Türk Kültür Vakfı terms" mark on `docs/credits.html`. **Do not deploy them without the foundation's written permission.** The melodies of these recordings are not transcribed into any corpus or bank.

The real recordings of 2026-10-28 are cut into notes by `scripts/samples/add_real_recordings.py` (pitch measured with pyin; a note holds within 40 cents for at least 0.4 s).
