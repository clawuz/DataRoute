# Gestures Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An articulation layer so a kemençe, a saz, a kanun, a nylon guitar, an upright bass, a tanpura and a ney are played with their own gestures.

**Architecture:** New pure module `globe/src/personal/gestures.ts`; `PersonalNote.glide`; the player/sampler glide path; the wiring in `compose.ts` after `humanize`. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-gestures-design.md` (read it first: it has every value).

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, vitest (`cd globe && npx vitest run`, `npx tsc --noEmit`).

## Global Constraints

- Deterministic; hashes via `routeHash` of `note.key + t + instrument`; no `Math.random`.
- Added notes are `show: false` and copy `route`, `via`, `key`, `alt`, `pan`, `wet` from their source note.
- An instrument without a gesture is untouched.
- Goldens (`globe/test/personal-episodes.test.ts`) re-captured only with `cd globe && python3 /tmp/probe/recap.py "<reason>"` after the failures are understood; the "no colour" rows keep their premise (`episodeCards` empty).
- Docs follow the code: `docs/music-data-mapping.md` (a "Gestures" section with the rows and a Where cell), `docs/music-instrument-idioms.md` (a "Status" line per gesture built).
- No push, no deploy.

### Task 5: Widen the gestures to the instruments and roles that are really used

The measurement after Task 3 (docs/music-instrument-idioms.md, "Measured after the first slice") shows that only the kemençe piece changed: the saz strum (threshold `vel >= 0.5`, the pluck role is 0.30–0.42), the kanun/santur tremolo (they play short pluck notes) and most instruments have no effect. Extend `gestures.ts` (pure, same rules, same context; roles come from `ctx.roleOf`: pad, bass, hook, pluck, kick, backbeat, hat, drone):

- **SAZ, BAGLAMA_MAKAM, BAGLAMA_EXTRA**: strum (−7 and −12 st at +12/+24 ms, d 0.14 s, vel ×0.4, hidden) on every **pluck**-role note that starts on a beat (within 20 ms of a multiple of `beat`) whatever its velocity; on hook-role notes keep `vel >= 0.5`.
- **KANUN, SANTUR**: pluck role: a **roll** on beat starts: hidden notes at +12 st (+35 ms) and +19 st (+70 ms), vel ×0.4; hook role: tremolo for `d >= 0.3` (was 0.45), octave as before.
- **HARP, FOLKHARP**: pluck role, first note of a bar: a rising hidden **glissando**: four chord tones (from `chordAt`, same octave, ascending) every 45 ms after the note, vel ×0.5.
- **PNO, STEINWAY, KAWAI, KNIGHTPNO, UPRIGHTPNO, EKEYS, RHODES, NS_EKEYS_*, KEYS_AC**: hook role: hidden octave doubling below (−12 st, vel ×0.35, same t and d) on notes that start on a beat; pluck role: on the first note of each bar a hidden left-hand bass note: chord root − 12 st, vel ×0.4, d 1.5 beats.
- **ACCORDION**: pluck and hook roles: **oom-pah**: on beats 0 and 2 of a bar a hidden bass note (chord root − 12, vel ×0.35, d 0.4 beat), on beats 1 and 3 a hidden stab of two chord tones (vel ×0.3, d 0.3 beat) (one set per bar position: do not add them once per note: dedupe by bar and beat).
- **VIOLIN**: hook role: `glide: 1` on the first note after a gap ≥ 0.3 s; a hidden double stop a perfect fifth below (−7, vel ×0.4) on notes with `d >= 0.5`.
- **KOTO, KAYAGEUM, DANTRANH, PSALTERY**: pluck role: a hidden repeat 60 ms later (vel ×0.5, same pitch); hook role: tremolo for `d >= 0.35`.
- **TENORSAX, CLA, FLUTE, OBOE, DUDUK, SHAKUHACHI, ERHU**: hook role: `glide: 1` on a note with `d >= 0.3` when `hash % 3 === 0`.
- **GUITAR_AC, GUITAR_EL, NS_GUITAR_AC_*, NS_GUITAR_EL_***: pluck role: strum like NYLONGUITAR (by card spread); card `country`: **boom-chick**: on beats 0 and 2 a hidden bass note (chord root − 12, vel ×0.5), the strum only on beats 1 and 3.
- **BAGPIPE**: a hidden grace note +2 st 40 ms before every note whose pitch equals the previous note's pitch in the same route (vel ×0.4, d 0.04 s).

Tests: one per new rule in `personal-gestures.test.ts` (synthetic notes), the composed-piece invariants of Task 3 stay (≤ 6000 notes: if a piece exceeds it, reduce the density of the rule that causes it, e.g. only every second beat, and say so), a coverage test: for the eight selections of docs/music-instrument-idioms.md the share of hidden notes is ≥ 10% in at least 6 of the 8 (measure first, then set the thresholds from what you see: honest numbers).
