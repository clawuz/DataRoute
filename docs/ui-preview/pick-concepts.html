Destination picker — concepts

Four modern ways to choose places for “Where I have been” and “Where I want to go”, instead of a long list of chips. Each keeps the night globe behind and the real Turkish Airlines routing (everything changes at Istanbul).

A · RECOMMENDED CORE

Search first (omnibox)

One big search field. Type a city, airport or country; suggestions appear with flag, code and country; tap to add. Selected places become chips. Empty state shows “Popular from London” and “Near you”.

Globe first

Pick on the globe itself: search flies the camera there, tap to drop a pin, pins draw their route live. A small bottom sheet holds the list. Most shareable, most “wow”, heavier on small phones.

D · PLAYFUL

Sentence builder

The screen is one sentence you complete: “I flew from London and visited …”. Tap a blank to search; each place becomes an editable word. Reads as a story, easy to share, works great on phones.
